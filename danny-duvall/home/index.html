The dilemma

The office of the evangelist has been almost absent.

Although the office of pastor and teacher is strong in the local modern church, the office of the evangelist has been almost absent for the last twenty years. Former means of evangelism have worked in some environments; however, protracted church revivals have failed in many communities to reach the lost.

Outward takes on the challenge of reaching the unchurched in your community by moving from a church service revival model to a relational equipping the saints model for the work of evangelism.

The Outward paradigm ignites the church in a perpetual way. Helping your people fulfill the Great Commission could prove to be one of the greatest church-health initiatives ever introduced.

“The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few.” Matthew 9:37 (KJV)

It is time for your church to take another step, turning Outward.

Outward's mission

To resource the church in its mission of the Great Commission by releasing the gift of evangelism.

Performance sheet · Year one

A two-year relationship with your church

If Outward becomes part of your church's plan to fulfill the Great Commission, year one delivers the following.

  1. First

    A relationship with you, the pastor

    This friendship is built around all things soul-winning, and driven by moving through the difficulties of transferring vision for lost souls. Nothing in Outward is more important than being in alignment with the pastor's heart. It all starts here.

  2. 4 weeks before

    A church-wide free digital book

    The Biopsy of a Soul: Somebody Save Me — How to Know When Someone Is Ready to Be Saved, by Danny DuVall, given to your entire church four weeks before Outward week. The church-wide read begins to stir the gift of evangelism and invites everyone to get involved.

  3. Sunday a.m.

    Evangelism workshop

    A 40-minute, verse-by-verse training on how Jesus intended His followers to share the gospel. Practical, fun, instructional and effective.

  4. Sunday p.m.

    Advanced evangelism workshop

    Sharpens the morning's skills and infuses your church with Spirit-enriched people skills carried beyond your walls, all with a soul-winning intention. Your church's hospitality grows in genuine, tangible, people-connecting ways.

  5. Monday night

    Sales · Souls · Purpose night

    The secret of Outward. Most churches find only about 5% of attenders test high in the gift of evangelism. Yet many people in your church have been professionally trained in sales, and that gifting lies dormant. Danny spent his life praying for souls and earnestly desiring spiritual gifts to reach them; God answered by sending him into six years of high-level professional sales training, where he saw how those skills transpose into reaching people for Jesus. This night shows Christian salespeople that what they have learned in their professional life is transferable to the Great Commission. Salespeople outside your church are invited too. This night is the game-changer.

  6. Tuesday night

    Transferable discipleship

    Sharing the gospel and making disciples are one and the same. Your people receive a discipleship path that produces their greatest year of personal spiritual growth and a vehicle they can give to others: doable, transferable and transformative. Never again will your people lead someone to the Lord and not know how to disciple them.

  7. Wednesday night

    Spirit-empowerment night

    A verse-by-verse biblical journey in Christ's command to be empowered by the Holy Spirit for one's personal mission for reaching souls. No music component and no emotional push, but direction at the start of the night on what will happen. There is opportunity to leave after the teaching, and those who stay are welcomed into a true Book of Acts encounter with Jesus, releasing empowerment for soul-winning into their life.

Beyond Outward week

The work continues.

NexGen evangelists

During Outward week Danny will look for, and ask you to help him find and meet, the young men and women sensing the call of God on their lives as evangelists. They are welcomed into a relationship with seasoned evangelists, including a monthly community video call with tenured evangelists and pastors on how to be effective in their calling. The NexGen group continues Outward in the life of your church in year two and beyond.

Every guest speaker, on mission

With your permission, Outward will call every scheduled guest for the next two years to discuss how they can contribute to your church's Outward focus, so every guest brings more value and your investment becomes even more valuable.

More evangelists, equipping the saints

Outward keeps connecting evangelists who share the vision and take responsibility for equipping the saints for the work of evangelism.

Year two

A second year of nurturing every deliverable above, so the intention of the mission is accomplished: resourcing your church's call to the Great Commission, and one more reason to help turn your church Outward.

The evangelist

Rev. Danny DuVall

Danny DuVall has been answering God's call since his days as an All-State quarterback in Cabot, Arkansas, where his faith in Christ was as clear off the field as on it. He grew up dreaming of coaching football. God had a different team in mind.

After playing quarterback on scholarship at Evangel College (now Evangel University) and earning a degree in Biblical Studies and Languages, Danny and his wife, Gayla, spent seventeen years traveling and ministering in churches across the nation. In 1995 they planted Christian Life Church in Birmingham, Alabama, which continues to thrive today.

An Assemblies of God minister, Danny serves as a teaching pastor, author and guest speaker to churches around the world. Six years of high-level professional sales training taught him how people decide, and he now teaches the church how to carry that skill to the Great Commission.
